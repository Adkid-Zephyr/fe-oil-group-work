# Interface brief
Self-contained local HTML, archived in the private group repository.
Reference: official anthropics/skills frontend-design/SKILL.md (read 2026-10-27), https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md . Public directory skills.sh lists widespread adoption; popularity is not a guarantee of design quality. No global skill installation.
Palette: paper #f7f5ef, ink #242821, muted #687067, line #d5d7ca, oil green #426953, terracotta #b36345.
Type: Georgia serif English display; system sans-serif body and numerical content. Offline font fallbacks.
Layout: generous left-aligned editorial research note with sticky section nav. One memorable hero: actual monthly oil price line and a clear research question. All major charts are functional and evidence-driven.
Wireframe: title + research thesis / observed price chart / three plain-language signals / rolling holdout evidence / coefficient decomposition / diagnostic evidence / desk briefing and editable notes / sources.
Review: user explicitly wants an Anthropic/prospectus feel, so warm paper and serif are intentional. Avoid fake logos, claim of official affiliation or invented prospectus. Use mixed full-width charts and prose rather than a repeated card grid. Dynamic controls explain model behaviour, not decorative animation.
Accessibility: semantic buttons, labels, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, mobile stacking, screen-readable tables and print layout.
